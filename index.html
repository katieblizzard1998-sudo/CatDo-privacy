Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026
Last updated: August 16, 2026
Applies to: CatDo Android closed beta (Google Play testing)

1. Who we are

CatDo (“Can do”) is a micro-task productivity app with cozy cat-home gamification. You complete small tasks during your open hours, earn in-app rewards (“PawBucks”), and furnish a virtual cat room.

CatDo is operated by Angelpet Studios, based in Casper, Wyoming, United States.

This Privacy Policy describes how the CatDo closed beta handles information when you use the Android app distributed through Google Play closed testing. This beta is not currently offered as a public website product, aside from this policy page.

2. Short version

Your CatDo progress and settings stay on your Android device. This beta does not use CatDo accounts, advertising, or third-party analytics trackers. Optional notifications are generated locally on your phone.

If you email us for support or feedback, or send a message through the in-app report form, we use the information you choose to send to troubleshoot issues, improve CatDo, and reply to you.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information stored on your device

CatDo saves app and gameplay data locally on your Android device using the app’s on-device storage. This may include:

  • Tasks you add and their status
  • PawBucks and other in-app rewards
  • Furniture you own and room progress
  • Your selected cat
  • Open-hours settings
  • Sound preferences, including your selected task-ping meow sound
  • Approximate play time
  • Whether a “rate us” prompt has already been shown (only used in non-beta builds, to decide when to ask for optional feedback)

This information is used to make CatDo function and remember your progress and preferences.

Because this information is stored locally, clearing CatDo’s app storage or uninstalling the app may permanently erase your progress. Android cloud backup of CatDo app data is disabled in this beta.

We do not currently sync your CatDo save to a CatDo cloud account.

4. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, CatDo may show local alerts when a task pings or a reward is ready to claim.

You can revoke CatDo’s notification permission at any time through your Android device settings.

This closed beta does not use a CatDo remote push-notification server. Notifications are generated locally and depend on Android notification permissions and the app’s ability to display local notifications.

5. Support and feedback messages

CatDo’s “Report a problem” feature lets you type a message in the app and send it to us. Sending a message is optional.

Anything you choose to include, such as a description of an issue, steps to reproduce it, or an optional reply email, is provided voluntarily by you.

We use support and feedback messages only to:

  • Troubleshoot problems
  • Respond to you
  • Understand bugs or usability issues
  • Improve CatDo

We do not sell information contained in your support or feedback messages.

In-app report delivery may be processed by our form/email delivery provider (Web3Forms) solely to deliver your message to our support inbox at catdosupport@gmail.com.

6. Information we do not collect through CatDo in this beta

CatDo does not currently collect or use:

  • Login accounts or passwords
  • Social sign-in information
  • Cloud backups of your CatDo save
  • Advertising identifiers for serving ads
  • Third-party analytics SDKs knowingly integrated by us
  • Real-money purchase or payment card information
  • Camera data
  • Contacts
  • Microphone recordings
  • Your photo library
  • Precise GPS location

Google Play and your Android device may independently process certain information, such as app installations, updates, device information, or crash and diagnostic information, under Google’s own policies. That processing is controlled by Google and the Android platform rather than CatDo.

7. How we use information

Information handled by CatDo is used to:

  • Provide CatDo’s gameplay and productivity features
  • Save your progress and preferences on your device
  • Show optional local task and reward notifications
  • Respond to support or feedback messages you choose to send
  • Improve CatDo’s stability and features based on tester feedback

8. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information.

We do not share your locally stored CatDo save with third parties.

If you send us a support or feedback message through the in-app form, that message may be processed by our form/email delivery provider as necessary to deliver it to our support inbox. We use the information you send only for the purposes described in this policy.

9. Retention

On-device data: CatDo data stored locally remains on your device until it is removed, such as when you clear CatDo’s app storage or uninstall the app.

Support and feedback emails: We retain support and feedback messages only for as long as reasonably necessary to address your request, troubleshoot issues, improve the beta, and maintain reasonable records. Messages may then be deleted or minimized when they are no longer needed.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate for a closed Android beta to protect information handled by CatDo.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in support or feedback emails unless it is necessary for us to understand or resolve your issue.

11. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • Clear CatDo’s app storage through Android settings to remove locally stored progress
  • Turn off CatDo notifications through Android settings
  • Uninstall CatDo to remove the app and its locally stored data
  • Choose not to send support or feedback messages
  • Contact us to request deletion of support or feedback messages you previously sent

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under applicable law.

To make a privacy request, contact catdosupport@gmail.com.

12. Children’s privacy

CatDo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, such as through a support email, please contact us at catdosupport@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

13. International users

CatDo’s closed beta is distributed through Google Play testing with a primary focus on users in the United States.

If you use CatDo from another country, information stored locally remains on your device as described in this policy. Any support or feedback emails you voluntarily send to us will be handled as described above.

14. Changes to this policy

CatDo is currently in closed beta, so its features and data practices may change as development continues.

We may update this Privacy Policy when CatDo changes. For example, we will update this policy if we introduce features such as accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, paid purchases, remote push notifications, a public website, or other features that materially change how information is handled.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this policy.
